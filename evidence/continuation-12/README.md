## Evidence and timing limits

Event-list times and selected-event navigation parameters in other screenshots are not automatically proof of execution time. The report identifies separate payload verification where available. Screenshots document their own captured view; they do not establish continuous foreground video coverage.

The quantity-three total of 3600 cents is an intentional fixture bug, not a Datadog defect.
