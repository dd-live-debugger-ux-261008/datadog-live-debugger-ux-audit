## Evidence limits

- Only genuine foreground motion from source 17 is used. Edits add opaque masks, native-scale crops, padding, explanatory text and chapter cuts. There are no inserted stills, reconstructed UI, synthesized motion or speed changes.
- The full source timeline is partitioned into retained and explicitly omitted intervals. The archive preserves all footage judged usable in this review. Identifying metadata-only views, private navigation, download-history screens and conservative transition margins are omitted. The highlight reel is selective.
- Work interruptions were reported for 19:57:30–19:59:16 and 20:27:43–20:30:08 UTC. The video also contains idle/tool waits and browser failures. The source duration must not be described as uninterrupted active testing.
- Chromium error 9 / Aw Snap and reload attempts around 20:24:30–20:27:14 UTC are browser-interruption evidence, not proof of a Datadog backend outage. An earlier browser error/reload is also retained where safe.
- This source contains active foreground QA throughout most of the recorded window; source 16’s late settled-page limitation does not apply wholesale. Only the actual captured foreground is shown; no background/direct-still evidence has been substituted.
- C1 baseline, C2 shifted-line capture, six repaired selected event stacks and editor revision warnings are visible. The main editor and selected event stack can differ. These clips do not by themselves establish complete test-case passes or eliminate outstanding attribution/cleanup requirements.
- The production-label draft was not submitted. Its blocked and cancelled state provides no result for that control.
- Fixture restart and later capture recovery require correlation with runtime receipts. Nominal Agent-outage and burst windows, and planned_phases/stop_agent/tiny_burst declarations in provider logs, do not establish actual execution. No Agent-outage or burst PASS is asserted.
- The F09 sequence shows another tab’s dirty draft resetting after a remote save. It does not establish that the stale write was accepted or that a hidden backend overwrite occurred.
- Unicode/cycle/empty-container observations show the captured interactions only. They do not establish a complete truncation, search/collapse or cycle-termination matrix. The Unicode-path installation error has a known direct-loader fixture confound.
- INACTIVE/DISABLED states and retained historical captures are UI evidence. GitHub Success is provider-workflow status. Neither alone proves the contents of every runtime-stage or owned-process cleanup receipt.
- Browser tabs, full URLs/parameters, account/avatar/session/service identities, private repository URLs, telemetry identifiers and repository-prefix values are cropped or opaque-masked. Broad masks intentionally hide adjacent ordinary UI. Synthetic revision SHAs may remain as technical evidence.
- UTC labels are derived from the recorder start. Provider clocks, UI event-list display times and SDK execution timestamps are distinct evidence types. The source has four timestamp gaps above the nominal 0.05-second step, with a maximum 0.20-second step; constant-20-fps output holds recorded frames across those gaps.
- Privacy verification combines sampled original-pixel and scene-change review, dense boundary checks, independent review, encoded samples, sampled OCR and complete decoding. It is sampling-based and is not a guarantee of every source pixel. Every final MP4 is silent and below 20,000,000 bytes.
